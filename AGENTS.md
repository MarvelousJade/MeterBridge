# Repository instructions

## Measure results and record resume evidence

Before implementation, read [docs/impact-and-resume-evidence.md](docs/impact-and-resume-evidence.md). Plan useful metrics, baseline and correctness checks before changing behavior. Preserve exact source revisions, environment, dataset, commands, all raw outcomes and limitations. Keep engineering, workflow and observed business impact separate. Maintain verified/preliminary/unverified claims and actual personal contribution. Never invent metrics; update an actual resume only when requested under its current instructions.
